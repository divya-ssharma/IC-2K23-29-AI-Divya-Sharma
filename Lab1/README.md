Roll No: IC-2K23-29
Water jug problem, vacuum cleaner and missionaries and cannibals.
Water jug is a fill and pour loop with a 4 litre and a 3 litre jug, target is 2 litres in either jug. The vacuum cleaner one is a reflex agent for two rooms, you type in the starting status of each room and where the vacuum is. Missionaries and cannibals is BFS over the valid states, boat carries at most 2, and the rule is that missionaries can never be outnumbered on either bank.
Missionaries and cannibals prints the whole state path. The other two just print the actions as they happen.
